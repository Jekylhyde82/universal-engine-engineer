# AFI OM + EVO PRIME — Intrinsic Integration Contract

**Parent architecture:** Universal Engine / Engineer  
**Status:** Foundational architecture requirement  
**Integration mode:** Intrinsic from inception; not modular add-ons

## 1. Purpose

AFI OM and EVO PRIME are fused fundamentally throughout Engineer. They retain distinct specialist responsibilities, but share the same architecture, intelligence environment, knowledge and memory foundations, interfaces, lifecycle, governance, evidence and verification controls.

This is a system-wide design invariant—not a proposal to install two independent subsystems.

## 2. Distinct responsibilities

### AFI OM — intelligence fusion and integration

AFI OM is responsible for:
- Acquiring and analysing relevant information and capability evidence.
- Extracting useful knowledge, methods, models, tools and implementation patterns.
- Reconciling conflicting information and preserving uncertainty and provenance.
- Synthesising intelligence across sources and specialist responsibilities.
- Assimilating and coordinating approved capabilities into Engineer's shared operating context.
- Distributing relevant intelligence to engineering, governance, memory, interfaces and decision processes.
- Detecting duplication, incompatibility, missing context and capability relationships.

### EVO PRIME — evolutionary engineering

EVO PRIME is responsible for:
- Identifying capability gaps, defects, bottlenecks and improvement opportunities.
- Translating verified intelligence into engineering requirements and design alternatives.
- Developing, adapting, optimising and testing capabilities.
- Evaluating compatibility, reliability, performance, maintainability and integration cost.
- Managing controlled change, rollback readiness and lifecycle transitions.
- Measuring outcomes and feeding observed results back into future improvement.
- Pursuing open-ended capability growth without bypassing safety, permissions, governance or evidence.

## 3. Intrinsic fusion across Engineer

AFI OM and EVO PRIME must be represented in the design and operation of every relevant architectural concern, including:

1. Identity and foundational architecture.
2. Intelligence, reasoning and decision support.
3. Discovery, research and investigation.
4. Capability acquisition, extraction and assimilation.
5. Knowledge, memory, context and provenance.
6. Engineering, implementation and orchestration.
7. Interfaces, connectivity, perception and workflows.
8. Security, privacy, permissions and governance.
9. Testing, evidence, independent verification and accountability.
10. Lifecycle, monitoring, adaptation and continuous evolution.

This is not a requirement to duplicate both responsibilities as separate code in every component. It means each component's contracts, data flows and lifecycle must natively support the relevant fusion and evolution responsibilities.

## 4. Continuous interaction

The core feedback loop is:

**DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → FUSE → IDENTIFY GAPS → DESIGN → SIMULATE → EVALUATE → VERIFY → APPROVE → INTEGRATE → MONITOR → LEARN → EVOLVE**

- AFI OM supplies synthesised, provenance-preserving intelligence and capability relationships.
- EVO PRIME converts verified findings into candidate engineering improvements and evaluates their outcomes.
- Results, failures, telemetry and new evidence return to AFI OM for reassessment and improved synthesis.
- The loop repeats under the same shared governance and evidence requirements.

Short form: **AFI OM fuses. EVO PRIME evolves. Engineer is the unified system.**

## 5. Governance and non-negotiable boundaries

Intrinsic integration does not grant unrestricted authority.

- Discovery does not grant permission to access, acquire, install, execute, publish or deploy.
- Third-party licences, terms, privacy and attribution obligations must be checked.
- Human approval is required for external commitments, spending, credential use, account creation, deployment and production changes.
- Unreviewed scripts, workflows and dependencies must not be executed.
- Security and safety controls apply throughout design and operation, not only at release time.
- A component cannot be the sole evaluator or certifier of its own correctness.
- Material claims require traceable evidence; unverified claims remain explicitly labelled.
- Rejected or retired capabilities remain recorded with reasons and provenance.

## 6. FOSS acquisition application

For the personal FOSS repository:

| Stage | AFI OM responsibility | EVO PRIME responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Fuse source intelligence and candidate evidence | Identify capability gaps and acquisition priorities |
| Capture | Preserve source identity, revisions and provenance | Define reproducible capture requirements |
| Assessment | Reconcile licence, dependency, security and quality findings | Evaluate technical fit, cost and maintainability |
| Design | Supply capability and dependency relationships | Design adapters, tests and integration boundaries |
| Approval | Present evidence, uncertainty and conflicts | Present expected benefit, risk and rollback plan |
| Integration | Assimilate approved capability metadata and knowledge | Implement and test approved integration |
| Verification | Maintain evidence traceability | Validate outcomes independently of the implementation |
| Evolution | Fuse operational findings and new discoveries | Improve, adapt or retire the integration under governance |

## 7. Architecture acceptance criteria

An implementation conforms only when:

- AFI OM and EVO PRIME responsibilities are built into the relevant system contracts and workflows from inception.
- Shared intelligence, provenance, memory and lifecycle information can flow between fusion and engineering processes.
- Their responsibilities are distinct without creating competing authorities or duplicated control planes.
- Candidate improvements pass security, licence, compatibility and verification gates.
- Integration and evolution decisions are traceable to evidence and approval.
- Failure, rollback, rejection and retirement are first-class lifecycle outcomes.
- No artificial evolutionary ceiling is imposed, while all legal, safety, permission and resource boundaries remain enforceable.

## 8. Governing principles

**EVERYTHING. FUNDAMENTALLY. INTRINSIC. ACROSS THE BOARD.**

**PROOF BEFORE CLAIM. EVIDENCE BEFORE CONFIDENCE. VERIFICATION BEFORE COMPLETION.**
