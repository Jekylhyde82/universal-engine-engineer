# UNIVERSAL ENGINE / ENGINEER
## Five-Pillar Multiversal Architecture with Intrinsic AFI OM Fusion
### Architecture specification v1.0 — proposed baseline

**Status:** Proposed architecture specification. This document defines design intent and contracts; it does not claim that every capability is implemented, tested, independently verified, or production-ready.

## 1. Architectural decision

Integrate the five selected Universal Engine / Engineer combinations into one coherent architecture, with **AFI OM intrinsically fused across all five**.

The five combinations are complementary capability domains, not five autonomous systems, five separate fusion authorities, or five competing governing bodies.

The unified architecture has:
- One governing architecture: **NINEFOLD INTRINSIC — MULTIVERSAL OMNI GOVERNANCE PRIME** (short name: **9 FOLD OMNI**).
- One intrinsically fused intelligence and memory foundation: **AFI OM × EB9 PRIME**.
- Five coordinated capability domains, described below.
- Nine UMIC execution contexts, retaining their existing count and isolation requirements.
- One canonical AFI PRIME fusion identity and function, with scoped instances; no additional fusion authority is created.

**Core principle:** one ecosystem, one governing architecture, one intrinsic intelligence-and-memory fusion foundation, five complementary capability domains, and independently verifiable outcomes.

## 2. Five integrated capability domains

### Domain 1 — OMNIVELOCITY PRIME GENIUS ENGINEER PRIME
**Mission:** high-capability reasoning, invention and engineering delivery.

Intrinsic responsibilities:
- GENIUS BRAINSTORM: generate candidate ideas, approaches and hypotheses.
- GENIUS CRITIC: challenge assumptions, identify defects, counterexamples and risks.
- GENIUS ENGINEER: translate approved requirements into architectures, designs, implementations and tests.
- AFI OM coordination: combine specialist contributions while preserving disagreement, provenance and confidence.

Acceptance boundary: idea generation is not approval; generated code or designs are not verified deliverables.

### Domain 2 — MULTIVERSAL OMNIVELOCITY PRIME GENIUS ENGINEER PRIME
**Mission:** explore, compare and engineer across alternative architectures, configurations, environments and possible futures.

Intrinsic responsibilities:
- Multiversal discovery and alternative generation.
- Cross-domain analysis and synthesis.
- Scenario modelling and simulation.
- Predictive estimation with uncertainty and assumptions made explicit.
- Parallel exploration and comparative evaluation.
- Engineering of selected alternatives after governance approval.

Acceptance boundary: “multiversal” denotes computational exploration of alternatives and possible futures. It does not claim physical access to other universes. Simulation and prediction are not observations or facts.

### Domain 3 — NINEFOLD INTRINSIC — MULTIVERSAL OMNI GOVERNANCE PRIME
**Mission:** govern the complete ecosystem and its lifecycle.

Nine governing responsibilities:
1. Genesis governance.
2. Architecture governance.
3. Intelligence governance.
4. Capability governance.
5. Operational governance.
6. Memory and knowledge governance.
7. Security and authority governance.
8. Assurance and verification governance.
9. Evolutionary governance.

This is the governing control plane, not an additional execution agent or fusion authority. It controls admission, policy, scope, lifecycle, evidence gates and change approval. It cannot grant itself unrestricted authority or self-certify its decisions.

### Domain 4 — ALPHA–GENESIS–OMEGA PRIME UNIVERSAL ENGINEER
**Mission:** foundational capability creation, first-principles development, optimisation and assurance.

Intrinsic responsibilities:
- ALPHA PRIME: foundational reasoning, capability primitives and first-principles analysis.
- GENESIS PRIME: discovery, creation and controlled introduction of new systems and capabilities.
- OMEGA PRIME: value, quality, completeness and outcome assurance.
- EVO PRIME: measured improvement, adaptation and reversible evolution.

Acceptance boundary: novelty and complexity are not measures of value. Proposals must demonstrate utility, feasibility, cost, risk and evidence.

### Domain 5 — QUANTUM–NEXUS–CHRONOS PRIME UNIVERSAL ENGINEER
**Mission:** deep systems analysis, relationship intelligence and temporal coordination.

Intrinsic responsibilities:
- QUANTUM PRIME: granular state, uncertainty and complex possibility analysis.
- NEXUS PRIME: dependency, relationship, interface and cross-system analysis.
- CHRONOS PRIME: temporal reasoning, sequencing, history and future-state analysis.
- META PRIME: evaluate and improve the methods used by the system itself.

TEMPORAL remains a distinct governance concern for scheduling, deadlines, temporal permissions, state transitions and continuity. It complements CHRONOS; it does not replace it.

Acceptance boundary: quantum terminology in this architecture describes computational analysis and modelling, not a claim of quantum-computing execution or quantum-physical capabilities.

## 3. Intrinsic AFI OM fusion across all five domains

**AFI OM is the shared intelligence-fusion foundation of all five domains. It is not a sixth domain, an optional plugin, or a bolt-on coordinator.**

Formal identity: **OMNI AFI PRIME — MULTIVERSAL NINEFOLD INTRINSIC FUSION** (short name: **AFI OM**).

AFI OM's nine intrinsic responsibilities remain:
1. AFI PRIME GENESIS LAB.
2. AFI PRIME OMNISIGHT.
3. AFI PRIME MULTIVERSAL SIMULATOR.
4. AFI PRIME UNIVERSAL ENGINEER.
5. AFI PRIME OMEGA ASSURANCE.
6. AFI PRIME UNIVERSAL INTELLIGENCE.
7. AFI PRIME UNIVERSAL ORCHESTRATOR.
8. AFI PRIME UNIVERSAL GOVERNANCE.
9. AFI PRIME OMEGA EVOLUTION.

These responsibilities provide shared fusion, specialist coordination, multimodal/perceptual development, simulation, engineering, assurance, orchestration and evolution services to all five domains. They do not create five copies of AFI OM or permit each domain to establish its own fusion authority.

### AFI OM × EB9 PRIME

**EB9 PRIME** (ELEPHANT BRAIN — NINEFOLD INTRINSIC PRIME) is intrinsically fused with AFI OM as its memory, knowledge and continuity foundation.

EB9 PRIME's nine responsibilities:
1. Memory acquisition.
2. Memory encoding.
3. Knowledge organisation.
4. Contextual intelligence.
5. Intelligent recall.
6. Memory reconciliation.
7. Continuity management.
8. Memory assurance.
9. Memory evolution.

AFI OM supplies coordinated intelligence and specialist fusion; EB9 PRIME supplies governed memory, knowledge and continuity. Their responsibilities remain distinguishable and traceable while operating as one integrated foundation.

### Shared fusion contract

Every domain may request AFI OM services through a versioned, typed contract containing at minimum:
- Request and correlation identity.
- Calling domain and target UMIC.
- Requested AFI OM responsibility/function and version.
- Authenticated principal reference and applicable policy/grant reference.
- Declared capability/provider requirements.
- Input provenance, sensitivity classification and payload limits.
- Deadline, resource budget and idempotency classification.
- Output schema, evidence requirements and failure semantics.

AFI OM must:
- Preserve each specialist result and its source.
- Preserve meaningful disagreement rather than manufacture consensus.
- Distinguish facts, hypotheses, predictions, simulations and recommendations.
- Carry uncertainty, confidence and provenance.
- Isolate provider failures and report partial outcomes.
- Respect per-UMIC scope, permissions and resource limits.
- Emit evidence references for consequential decisions and actions.
- Refuse or defer requests that lack authority, policy approval or required evidence.

EB9 PRIME must:
- Preserve provenance, timestamps, version and context for retained knowledge.
- Reconcile conflicting memories without silent overwrite.
- Mark superseded or stale information while retaining the audit trail.
- Enforce retention, access and deletion policies.
- Support continuity and recovery without inventing missing history.

## 4. Coordination and authority model

The five domains do not have equal governance authority.

- **9 FOLD OMNI** is the governing control plane.
- **AFI OM × EB9 PRIME** is the shared intelligence, fusion, memory and continuity foundation, operating under 9 FOLD OMNI policy.
- Domains 1, 2, 4 and 5 are complementary intelligence/engineering capability domains.
- Domain 3 is the governance domain and retains the ecosystem-wide policy and lifecycle mandate.
- UMICs remain execution contexts with isolated state and scoped permissions. They are not additional governing bodies.

No domain may bypass governance by invoking AFI OM directly with broader permissions. AFI OM does not confer authority; it executes only within authenticated grants and policy.

## 5. Integration model

The integration is expressed as contracts, not a presumption that every pair needs a bespoke agent or permanent process.

For the five domains, define:
- Five domain-to-AFI OM service contracts.
- Five domain-to-EB9 PRIME memory/continuity contracts, mediated through AFI OM and policy.
- Governance policy and lifecycle gates applying to all domain interactions.
- Cross-domain interfaces for approved hand-offs, shared evidence and dependency coordination.

Where useful, the nine governing responsibilities and nine AFI OM responsibilities may be mapped against the five domains. Such mappings must be explicit, versioned and tested. A matrix cell is an interface/relationship, not automatically a new agent, process, service, or authority.

## 6. Lifecycle and verification gates

New capabilities follow:
**Discovered → Proposed → Under Evaluation → Approved → Integrated / Rejected / Retired.**

Required gates:
1. Identity, owner and intended responsibility established.
2. Canonical architecture and register mapping checked.
3. Interfaces, dependencies and version compatibility checked.
4. Security, privacy, authority and supply-chain review completed.
5. Resource and performance budgets defined and measured.
6. Acceptance tests and failure/recovery tests passed.
7. Evidence provenance and independent verification recorded.
8. Rollback, retirement and migration path defined.

Engineering cannot certify itself. The independent verifier must be able to disagree, reject evidence or block release. Historical test results do not establish current-head status.

## 7. Invariants

- No artificial ceiling on discovery, capability ambition or evolutionary design.
- No claim of unlimited compute, resources, permissions or physical access.
- Discovery is not approval; approval is not integration; integration is not verification.
- Simulation is not real-world evidence; prediction is not fact.
- No silent memory overwrite or fabricated continuity.
- No implicit cross-UMIC permission inheritance.
- No extra UMIC, duplicate AFI fusion authority or hidden parallel governance.
- Complexity is justified by measurable capability or value.
- All consequential actions are attributable, permissioned, logged and recoverable where technically feasible.

## 8. Implementation status and next engineering sequence

This specification is a proposed architecture baseline. It does not assert that the five domains or their complete AFI OM fusion are implemented.

Recommended sequence:
1. Reconcile this specification with existing 9 FOLD OMNI registers and the current AFI PRIME runtime integration plan.
2. Define the canonical domain IDs, ownership, versioning and interfaces.
3. Define and validate the five AFI OM request/response contracts and EB9 PRIME memory contracts.
4. Map each domain to existing AFI OM responsibilities without duplicating fusion authority.
5. Implement a thin integration proof using the existing provider-neutral coordinator and nine-UMIC runtime boundaries.
6. Add isolation, permission, replay, timeout, partial-failure, provenance, memory-conflict and independent-verifier tests.
7. Run CI on the resulting PR head and report exact evidence.
8. Keep the PR in draft and do not merge or deploy without explicit approval.

**Release posture:** NO-GO for production until implementation, security review, independent verification, target-device/resource assessment and current-head CI demonstrate the required properties.
