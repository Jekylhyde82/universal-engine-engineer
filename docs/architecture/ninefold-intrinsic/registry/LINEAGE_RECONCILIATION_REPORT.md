## 6. Required source-recovery method

A lineage may be marked mapped only when its record includes:

1. exact canonical name as recorded in an approved source;
2. exact responsibility and scope;
3. source repository/document/conversation reference and source version;
4. owner approval or an explicit decision reference;
5. destination lineage ID and UMIC assignment;
6. relationship to any historic or superseded identity;
7. duplicate/conflict review;
8. evidence that the mapping does not double-count or erase a constituent capability;
9. independent reconciliation review.

Where a source is missing, conflicting or ambiguous, preserve the placeholder and mark the mapping unresolved. A proposed taxonomy may be evaluated separately, but it must never be represented as recovered historical fact.
