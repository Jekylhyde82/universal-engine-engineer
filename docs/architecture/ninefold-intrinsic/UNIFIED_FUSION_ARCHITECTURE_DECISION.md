# NINEFOLD INTRINSIC — Unified Fusion Architecture Decision

**Decision ID:** NFI-ADR-0001  
**Authority:** NINEFOLD INTRINSIC  
**Decision status:** Adopted architectural direction; implementation and independent verification pending  
**Fusion authority:** UNIFIED FUSION SYNERGY — AFI PRIME  
**Execution governance:** DESIGNATED DRIVER (D.D.)  
**Perception governance:** V.I.S.I.O.N.  
**Evidence discipline:** PROOF BEFORE CLAIM. EVIDENCE BEFORE CONFIDENCE. VERIFICATION BEFORE COMPLETION.

## 1. Decision

NINEFOLD INTRINSIC is to be represented as **one unified entity with one canonical identity and one intrinsically integrated intelligence architecture**.

The architecture contains **nine unified UMICs**, each formed by integrating its one Master Core lineage and nine Engine lineage slots through one scoped instance of the same canonical **Unified Fusion Synergy — AFI PRIME** function. The nine unified UMICs are then intrinsically fused and governed as **one NINEFOLD INTRINSIC entity**.

There is one canonical fusion-function identity and nine scoped per-UMIC fusion-core instances. These are not nine competing fusion systems or nine top-level entities; they are nine governed instances of one shared fusion architecture.

The previous model of AFI PRIME and Fusion Synergy as separate current architecture components is also superseded. They are one unified fusion function and one current architecture identity. Their historical provenance and constituent capability contributions remain traceable.

## 2. Canonical structure

The architecture contains these preserved capability lineages:

| Lineage | Count | Treatment after fusion |
|---|---:|---|
| Former UMIC domains | 9 | Preserved as nine internal specialist domains, not nine independent top-level entities |
| Master Core capability lineages | 9 | Preserved as internal core capabilities and responsibilities |
| Engine capability lineages | 81 | Preserved as individually identifiable specialist capabilities |
| **Total preserved constituent lineages** | **99** | Integrated within one unified entity |

The count of 99 refers to the preserved UMIC-domain, Master-Core and Engine lineages. The nine scoped fusion-core instances are not additional peer lineages: they are per-UMIC instances of one shared canonical fusion function. Each instance integrates one Master Core slot and nine Engine slots within its UMIC; the nine resulting unified UMICs form one entity. Exact source-to-slot lineage mapping remains pending reconciliation and must not be fabricated.

## 3. Meaning of fusion

Fusion means that the constituent capabilities are designed and governed as one coherent intelligence architecture rather than as independent top-level intelligence systems connected by interfaces.

Fusion means each UMIC's Master Core and nine Engine slots are internally unified through its scoped instance of the shared AFI PRIME fusion function, then the nine unified UMICs are fused into one coherent entity. Fusion does not mean erasing specialist expertise, flattening all responsibilities into one generic function, or removing internal security and accountability boundaries.

The architecture must preserve:

- each of the nine former UMIC domain identities as traceable internal domains;
- each Master Core's defined capability, responsibility and provenance;
- each of the eighty-one Engines' specialist purpose, interfaces, evidence and lifecycle;
- the ability to inspect, test, update, disable, replace or roll back a bounded internal capability without fragmenting the unified identity;
- cross-domain synthesis and shared intelligence through Unified Fusion Synergy — AFI PRIME.

No constituent lineage is to be treated as a separate competing UMIC or independent top-level intelligence identity after adoption of this decision.

## 4. Unified Fusion Synergy — AFI PRIME

Unified Fusion Synergy — AFI PRIME is one intrinsic fusion function, not two adjacent components.

Its unified responsibilities include:

1. **Analysis and evaluation:** examine requirements, evidence, alternatives, constraints and uncertainty.
2. **Critical reasoning:** detect contradictions, unsupported claims, missing evidence and failure modes.
3. **Specialist coordination:** invoke and coordinate the appropriate internal specialist capabilities.
4. **Synthesis:** combine findings and capabilities into coherent cross-domain outcomes.
5. **Integration:** maintain compatible shared models, interfaces, policies and capability relationships.
6. **Conflict resolution:** identify conflicting outputs, preserve provenance and route unresolved conflicts through governance.
7. **Evolution assessment:** evaluate proposed new capabilities against lifecycle, security, value and compatibility requirements.
8. **Traceability:** preserve which internal capability lineages and evidence contributed to a result.

Unified Fusion Synergy — AFI PRIME does not grant itself permissions, approve its own high-risk actions, or independently certify its own results.

## 5. Internal modularity, resilience and fault containment

A single identity must not become a fragile monolith.

The implementation must preserve internal modularity and bounded fault domains while maintaining one unified architecture. The fusion function may be logically singular while its implementation is redundant, replicated or distributed where evidence shows that doing so improves resilience.

Required safeguards:

- no avoidable single point of failure in critical fusion paths;
- bounded failure domains and graceful degradation;
- explicit timeouts, cancellation and resource limits;
- versioned internal contracts and compatibility checks;
- capability isolation and least privilege;
- recovery, rollback and state-reconciliation procedures;
- observability sufficient to trace decisions without indiscriminate collection of sensitive data;
- deterministic handling of duplicate, stale, conflicting or incomplete evidence.

A failure or quarantine of one internal capability must not silently corrupt the whole unified entity.

## 6. Governance and independent verification

Unity of identity does not confer unlimited authority.

- Governance remains an independent control plane.
- Security policy and permission boundaries remain enforceable across all internal domains.
- D.D. may execute only bounded, authorised actions and cannot expand its own authority.
- V.I.S.I.O.N. may observe and interpret only explicitly authorised visual inputs. Observation is not consent or execution authority.
- High-risk, destructive, irreversible, financial, external-publication, credential, permission and production-release actions remain subject to explicit approval.
- The fusion function, its executor and the capability being evaluated cannot be the sole verifier of their own success.
- Independent verification must inspect evidence and postconditions against the exact artefact, action and policy version.

## 7. D.D. and V.I.S.I.O.N. responsibilities

### DESIGNATED DRIVER (D.D.)

D.D. is the governed execution delegate for approved engineering work. Its responsibility is to translate authorised plans into bounded actions, record what was attempted, reconcile uncertain outcomes and provide evidence for independent verification.

D.D. is not an unrestricted autonomous authority. A plan, recommendation or successful tool call does not itself establish that the intended outcome is correct.

### V.I.S.I.O.N.

V.I.S.I.O.N. is the governed perception and visual-understanding capability. Its intended scope includes authorised screen or image observation, spatial/context interpretation and reporting uncertainty.

No live camera, screen-sharing, Android capture or device-control capability is implied by this architecture decision. Those require a separately implemented and permission-gated integration, with consent, minimisation, redaction, threat testing and device validation.

### Joint workflow

1. Unified Fusion Synergy — AFI PRIME analyses the requirement and prepares a critically reviewed plan.
2. V.I.S.I.O.N. supplies only authorised observations and labels uncertainty.
3. D.D. executes only actions covered by explicit authority and policy.
4. An independent verifier checks exact postconditions and evidence.
5. EB9PRIME may preserve only approved, provenance-bearing records under its retention and correction controls.

## 8. Lifecycle for capability evolution

New capabilities do not enter the unified entity merely because they are discovered.

The lifecycle remains:

**Discovered → Proposed → Under Evaluation → Approved → Integrated / Rejected / Retired**

Integration requires documented value, provenance, licence, security, compatibility, resource, governance and proof-of-capability assessment.

The unified architecture must not have a fixed evolutionary ceiling, but every integration remains subject to evidence and governance.

## 9. Required acceptance criteria

The architecture is not implementation-complete until all applicable criteria are demonstrated:

1. **Identity integrity:** one canonical NINEFOLD INTRINSIC identity; nine unified UMICs are internal domains, not competing top-level entities.
2. **Lineage preservation:** all 9 UMIC-domain, 9 Master-Core and 81 Engine capability lineages remain traceable.
3. **Nine scoped fusion cores:** each UMIC has one scoped instance of the shared fusion function, one Master Core slot and nine Engine slots.
4. **Single fusion definition:** one canonical Unified Fusion Synergy — AFI PRIME identity and responsibility definition; nine governed instances, not duplicate AFI/Fusion components.
5. **Cross-domain synthesis:** tested examples demonstrate coherent use of multiple unified UMICs and specialist Engines.
6. **Specialist accountability:** each internal capability has an owner/responsibility, contract, evidence path and lifecycle status.
7. **Fault containment:** failure injection demonstrates that an internal capability failure does not corrupt or disable the entire unified entity.
8. **Security and authority:** tests show that internal fusion cannot bypass policy, permissions, approvals or data boundaries.
9. **Independent verification:** verification is performed by a genuinely separate authority and is bound to exact artefacts and postconditions.
10. **Evolution and rollback:** a new capability can be evaluated, integrated, rejected or retired with provenance and safe rollback.

Passing documentation checks alone is not sufficient. Each criterion requires implementation evidence appropriate to its risk.

## 10. Migration and reconciliation requirements

Before this decision is treated as the repository-wide canonical architecture:

- locate every active architecture document, registry, diagram, schema and code contract that describes UMICs, Master Cores, Engines, AFI PRIME or Fusion Synergy;
- identify conflicting or obsolete formulations, including nine independent UMICs and separate AFI/Fusion components;
- update canonical definitions and cross-references consistently;
- preserve historical records as superseded history rather than silently rewriting provenance;
- reconcile registry counts and ensure the 9 UMICs, 9 Master Core lineages and 81 Engine lineages are represented exactly once, with nine scoped fusion-core instances and one final entity;
- add schema and automated consistency checks where registries are machine-readable;
- run tests against the exact resulting commit;
- obtain independent review before merging or promoting the architecture.

This ADR records the decision and intended migration. It does not assert that all repository references, runtime components or registries have already been migrated.
