# MULTIVERSAL OMNIVELOCITY PRIME — GENIUS ENGINEER PRIME

**Status:** Architecture baseline — proposed for review  
**Architecture foundation:** NINEFOLD INTRINSIC  
**Universal governing invariant:** INTRINSIC BY DESIGN

## 1. Purpose
Establish one coherent, modular engineering architecture for discovering, evaluating, integrating, operating, verifying and evolving capabilities across multiple domains and environments. This records the approved direction; it does not claim implementation.

## 2. Canonical identity
The canonical identity is **MULTIVERSAL OMNIVELOCITY PRIME — GENIUS ENGINEER PRIME**. It is one integrated identity, not two parallel systems.

- **MULTIVERSAL:** cross-domain and cross-system scope.
- **OMNIVELOCITY PRIME:** an objective for adaptive, coordinated capability and efficient movement between problem spaces; not a claim of unlimited speed or capability.
- **GENIUS ENGINEER PRIME:** the governing discipline for discovery, analysis, criticism, design, implementation, testing, independent verification and controlled evolution.

## 3. Foundational invariants
1. **INTRINSIC BY DESIGN:** compatible responsibilities and capabilities are accommodated from the outset, not bolted on later.
2. **NINEFOLD INTRINSIC:** the foundational organisational architecture.
3. **No evolutionary ceiling:** capabilities may be discovered indefinitely; integration remains governed.
4. **OMEGA PRIME Intelligence Value Invariant:** verified usefulness takes precedence over impressive complexity.
5. **U.C.P.C.I.:** configuration and capability parity discovery, not impossible exhaustive enumeration.
6. **Evidence before confidence:** consequential claims require traceable evidence.
7. **Independent verification:** an implementation cannot certify itself merely by repeating its own checks.
8. **Modularity:** intrinsic accommodation does not mean monolithic construction or simultaneous execution of every capability.
9. **Explicit dependencies:** external services, models, libraries, devices and infrastructure must be identified, versioned, governed and tested.

## 4. Integrated responsibilities
These are responsibilities within one architecture, not automatically separate deployable agents or divisions:

- GENIUS BRAINSTORM — generate options and hypotheses.
- GENIUS CRITIC — challenge assumptions, evidence and failure modes.
- GENIUS ENGINEER — design and implement.
- GENESIS PRIME — foundational coherence and controlled creation.
- ALPHA PRIME — initiation and foundational coordination.
- OMEGA PRIME — value, completeness and outcome integrity.
- TEMPORAL PRIME — time, sequencing, history and change governance.
- EVO PRIME principles — capability discovery, evaluation, controlled integration, adaptation and improvement.

## 5. Capability lifecycle
Every candidate capability follows:

**Discovered → Proposed → Under Evaluation → Approved → Integrated / Rejected / Retired**

Discovery is not approval. Approval is not implementation. Implementation is not verification. Each transition records authority, rationale, evidence, affected components and resulting version.

These are design targets, not evidence that inventories exist or are complete. Each record needs a stable identifier, owner, purpose, status, version, provenance and relevant cross-references. Do not invent entries to satisfy counts.

## 7. Architecture constraints
- Prefer small, independently testable components with explicit contracts.
- Define trust boundaries, permissions and data classifications before connecting external systems.
- Use least privilege and deny-by-default for sensitive operations.
- Require human approval for foundational, destructive, financial, privacy-sensitive or externally consequential actions.
- Make operations observable, auditable and recoverable.
- Require rollback or compensating-action plans for consequential changes.
- Keep secrets out of source control and logs.
- Treat external content and tool output as untrusted input.

## 8. Required evidence before production
A production release requires a reproducible build and locked dependencies; automated unit, integration, security and regression tests; threat model; authentication and authorisation controls; secret management; logging, metrics and health checks; backup and restore demonstration; deployment and rollback procedure; resource limits and failure handling; privacy and retention procedures; independent evidence review; and operator runbooks.

## 9. Status vocabulary
- **DOCUMENTED:** described in an authoritative artifact.
- **PLANNED:** intended but not implemented.
- **IMPLEMENTED:** code or configuration exists.
- **TESTED:** specified tests executed with results attached.
- **VERIFIED:** evidence reviewed against explicit criteria by an appropriately independent reviewer.
- **PRODUCTION-APPROVED:** all release gates passed and an authorised decision recorded.

Never promote status based on intention or narrative alone.
