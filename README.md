# Universal Engine / Engineer

**Core purpose:** A purpose-built, self-upgrading and evolving AI engineering system.

> Build for capability today. Design for possibilities tomorrow. Engineer for evolution indefinitely.

## Intrinsic architecture: AFI OM + EVO PRIME

**AFI OM and EVO PRIME are fundamentally intrinsic to Engineer from inception.** They are not optional plugins, bolt-on modules, detached supervisory layers, competing systems or separate products. Their principles, responsibilities and feedback loops are woven throughout the architecture.

- **AFI OM — intelligence fusion and integration:** analyses, extracts, synthesises, assimilates, coordinates and distributes intelligence, knowledge and capabilities across Engineer.
- **EVO PRIME — evolutionary engineering:** discovers gaps and opportunities, designs improvements, develops and tests capabilities, optimises performance, adapts the architecture and drives continuous, governed improvement.

Their responsibilities remain distinguishable while operating as one mutually reinforcing architecture:

**AFI OM fuses intelligence and capability. EVO PRIME engineers and evolves the system. Each continuously informs and improves the other.**

This intrinsic integration applies across:
- Identity, architecture, reasoning and decision support
- Discovery, research, investigation and capability acquisition
- Knowledge, memory, context and information retrieval
- Design, implementation, orchestration and engineering
- Interfaces, connectivity, perception and operational workflows
- Security, privacy, permissions, governance and accountability
- Testing, evidence, independent verification and quality assurance
- Lifecycle management, adaptation, optimisation and evolution

No domain is exempt by default. New capabilities must be assessed for architectural integration rather than automatically attached.

## Governing lifecycle

**DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → DESIGN → SIMULATE → EVALUATE → VERIFY → APPROVE → INTEGRATE → MONITOR → EVOLVE**

Discovery never automatically grants permission to integrate or execute. Approval, security controls, provenance and independent verification remain intrinsic to the system.

## FOSS capability acquisition

The personal FOSS repository is located in [`foss/`](foss/README.md). It is governed by the same AFI OM + EVO PRIME architecture:

- AFI OM evaluates and fuses source intelligence, provenance, licence facts, dependencies, capabilities and evidence.
- EVO PRIME identifies capability gaps, designs adaptations, tests improvements and evolves approved integrations.
- Neither role can bypass licence, security, permission or verification gates.
- Third-party source identity and attribution remain preserved.

## Engineering invariants

- **EVERYTHING. FUNDAMENTALLY. INTRINSIC. ACROSS THE BOARD.**
- Every capability is assessed; discovery is not approval.
- Every evolution is governed and evidence-backed.
- No component is its own sole verifier.
- No artificial evolutionary ceiling is imposed; evolution remains bounded by safety, permissions, law, evidence and available resources.
- **PROOF BEFORE CLAIM. EVIDENCE BEFORE CONFIDENCE. VERIFICATION BEFORE COMPLETION.**
